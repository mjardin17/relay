StoryForge: Algorithmic Narrative Coherence Engine

Graph-Based Plot Continuity & Manuscript Generation

StoryForge is an exploratory research engine addressing one of the hardest challenges in generative AI: maintaining coherent character motivation, spatial world-rules, and causal plot continuity across 80,000+ word manuscripts.

### Graph Architecture Rather than treating long-form prose as an unstructured token sequence, StoryForge represents character knowledge states, relationship matrices, and world bible rules as an explicit constraint graph. Each generated chapter is evaluated against the graph before acceptance.
