Relay Architecture: Deterministic AI Operating System

Production Dogfood & Operational Architecture Deep-Dive

Relay is Jardin’s Outpost’s flagship autonomous business operating system, currently deployed and operating in production dogfood for trade business workloads.

### Architectural Thesis Modern AI agent architectures fail when they allow non-deterministic LLMs to make direct mutations to production state, publish unverified content, or operate without human approval gates. Relay solves this through three non-negotiable architectural pillars:

1. **Cryptographic Segregation of Duties (SoD):** AI agents may compose, propose, and validate operations, but cannot approve publication or live deployments. Dual-key authorization is cryptographically enforced. 2. **Controlled Integration Testing:** The platform is validated with deterministic contract tests across 155 tests passing across 46 suites, executing real SQLite operations and HTTP pipelines. 3. **Deterministic State & Schema Generation:** Web presence compilation transforms verified business facts into static, accessible HTML with Schema.org JSON-LD and zero hallucinated claims.
