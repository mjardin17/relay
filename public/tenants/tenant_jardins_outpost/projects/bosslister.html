BossLister: Resale Intelligence & Multi-Channel Sync

Automated Catalog Ingestion & Pricing Valuation Architecture

BossLister is a specialized commerce intelligence platform designed to eliminate the manual bottleneck in high-throughput secondary market resale.

### Core Engineering 1. **Multi-Attribute Ingestion:** Extracts and standardizes condition, material, brand, model, and year data across heterogeneous product catalogs. 2. **Valuation Engine:** Computes real-time market comparables and estimated sell-through velocity. 3. **Cross-Platform Schema Formatting:** Automatically compiles marketplace-compliant listing schemas for rapid inventory distribution.
