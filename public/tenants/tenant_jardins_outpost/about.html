ORIGIN & PHILOSOPHY

A craftsman’s workshop for
working software.

Jardin’s Outpost is an independent digital workshop where practical tools get engineered directly from real-world problems in online selling, content distribution, narrative creation, and small-business operations.

1. Tools built from friction I actually felt

Software shouldn’t start in a boardroom with buzzwords and market research templates. Every system inside Jardin’s Outpost started as an actual bottleneck: spending hours manually copying listings to three different marketplaces, fighting plot holes in complex story outlines, or trying to format one video across six social networks without losing quality.

2. Deterministic execution over AI fluff

AI is powerful, but only when bounded by deterministic data structures, strict state machines, and verified guardrails. If an item sells on eBay, the system must immediately and reliably lock the inventory across other channels. If a book chapter contradicts character lore, the story graph must catch it before compilation.

3. Clean, independent product boundaries

Just because products can talk to each other doesn’t mean they should be mashed into an unmaintainable monolith. StoryForge is for writing and narrative universe packaging. BossLister is for resale product intelligence. Crosspost is for multi-platform distribution. They are independent tools that connect through clean, modular handoffs.
