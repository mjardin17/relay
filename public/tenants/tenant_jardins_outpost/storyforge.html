The Problem

Writers spend 80% of their energy on marketing friction.

Writing a book is only half the battle. Authors must constantly track timeline contradictions, format EPUBs, create blurb variations, edit vertical TikTok hooks, and assemble promotion packages across platforms.

The Solution

Algorithmic story graph + automated promo suite.

StoryForge parses character arcs and timeline nodes to eliminate plot holes, compiles print/digital files, and generates ready-to-post short-form video teaser scripts with audio stems.
