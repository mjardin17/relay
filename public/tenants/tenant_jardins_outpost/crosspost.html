MULTI-CHANNEL DISTRIBUTION ENGINE

Create once.
Distribute everywhere with zero drift.

Crosspost is the multi-platform distribution product with two dedicated surfaces: Crosspost Media for social content and Crosspost Commerce for marketplace inventory.

Explore Crosspost Media ↓ Explore Crosspost Commerce ↓
Architecture Guardrail: Crosspost is a standalone distribution engine. It receives finished video/social media payloads from StoryForge, and structured listing inventory payloads from BossLister.
SURFACE 01 • CONTENT DISTRIBUTION

Crosspost Media

One piece of content adapted for every major video and text social channel with platform-specific captions, aspect ratios, tags, and queue tracking.

Automated Ratio & Trim Adaptation

Formats 9:16 vertical videos for TikTok & Shorts, 1:1 square cards for Instagram, and widescreen previews for X and LinkedIn.

Native Caption Styling

Generates platform-tailored character-safe titles, hashtag clusters, and engagement prompts without awkward auto-formatting errors.

Queue & Telemetry Engine

Queues drafts, schedules timed releases, monitors publishing status, and logs first-party response analytics.

Instagram VERIFIED

SURFACE 02 • MULTI-MARKETPLACE COMMERCE

Crosspost Commerce

One product listing adapted for multiple marketplaces with synchronized inventory, price controls, and instant anti-oversell locks.

Instant Anti-Oversell Protection

When an item sells on eBay, Crosspost Commerce instantly sends withdrawal signals to Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, and Poshmark within seconds.

Marketplace Category Taxonomy

Translates item conditions, brands, sizes, and specs into the unique taxonomy required by each marketplace API.

Live Inventory State Machine

Tracks listing status (Draft, Queued, Active, Sold, Delisted) with an audit trail and fail-safe retry handlers.
