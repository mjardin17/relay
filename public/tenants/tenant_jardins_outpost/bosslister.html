End-to-End Execution

The BossLister Product Intelligence Flow

01. SCAN

Photo / Barcode

Snap 2–4 photos of the item or scan its UPC/model tag directly from a mobile browser.

02. IDENTIFY

Product Match

Computer vision matches brand, exact model number, year, condition grade, and colorway.

03. COMPS

Sold Research

Queries historical sold data across eBay, Mercari, and Poshmark to establish fair market value.

04. FEES & PROFIT

Margin Engine

Deducts platform commission, estimated shipping, and cost-of-goods to show true net take-home profit.

05. LISTING

Channel Payload

Formats descriptions, item specifics, and photos, then sends verified payload to Crosspost Commerce.

01

Fast Sourcing Decisions

Know within 5 seconds at an estate sale or thrift store if an item is worth buying based on real sold velocity and estimated margin.

02

Accurate Item Specifics

Populates mandatory eBay aspects, UPC numbers, material types, and measurements without manual typing.

03

Direct Export Handshake

Seamlessly bundles photos, price thresholds, and descriptions into a clean JSON payload for Crosspost Commerce multi-market posting.
